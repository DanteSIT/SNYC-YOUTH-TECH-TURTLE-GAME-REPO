## Findings

1. **Boss arc is correct.** Boss encounters exist exactly at levels 5, 10, 15.
2. **Monotonic difficulty ramp holds.** Density climbs 0.79 → 3.33 across 15 levels with a
   genuine Rest dip now at 8–9. Gate height variety ramps too: level_1 gates float at y 1.5–2.9,
   later levels mix low (0.5) and high gates for vertical figure-eights.
3. **Rest phase breather (RESOLVED).** Levels 8–9 were as dense as level 7 (2.08); both were
   pulled down to 1.67 (one gate + one hazard removed from each) so the pre-crescendo beat reads.
4. **Tutorial difficulty (RESOLVED).** tutorial.tscn originally carried 3 hazards — more threat
   than level_1/level_2. Removed Hazard2 and Hazard3, keeping the single teaching encounter.
5. **Length is flat.** All levels are ~48 m (span collapsed from the original 46/80/120 ladder).
   Pacing now lives entirely in density, not distance.
6. **Collectable economy flat.** 17→11 jelly across the run; rewards don't grow with risk.
7. **Fish schools flat at 4/level**, reef clusters 1/level — scenery density does not escalate.

## Recommendations (remaining, not applied)

- Optional: add 1–2 extra foreground fish schools and a second reef cluster on levels 12–15.
- Optional: convert 1–2 jelly into higher-value pickups on late levels (economy reward escalation).
- Length ladder (world-spans 46/80/120) would require a `gen_levels.py` CONFIGS overhaul plus
  a rebuild of the hand-frozen scenes — tracked as a follow-up, not done here.

## Changes applied (this audit)

- `level_8.tscn`, `level_9.tscn`: removed `Gate3`/`Gate0` and `Hazard2` to create the Rest dip
  (density 2.08 → 1.67). No collectables, pipes, or scenery touched.
- `tutorial.tscn`: removed `Hazard2` and `Hazard3` (the stacked teaching trio), keeping a single
  `Hazard1` encounter (hazards 3 → 1).
- All 16 scenes re-validated after the edits (see `tools/pipeline/validate.sh`).
