#  WHAT WAS WRONG. Two separate defects, both reported by eye as "the floor is

#  Two causes, and fixing only the obvious one would have looked like a fix

#
#    * shark_boss.gd assigns $Body.rotation.y = sin(...) * 0.15 every physics

#      180 degree turn in shark_boss.tscn never applied. Both rotation

#      Result 4.8 degrees.
#
#    * zshark.glb faces +Z, NOT the -Z the glTF convention implies. Measured:
#      the caudal fin is a tall laterally thin plate (X 0.088, Y 1.153) at
#      local z = -3.90, aspect 13.1 against 5.3 for the runner-up, and the
#      head is at the +Z end. The original diag_shark_facing.gd used "deepest
#      slab is the head" -- that slab is the PECTORAL FINS -- and passed both
#      the right model and a backwards one, so it was deleted rather than
#      trusted. That heuristic now lives in probe_boss_facing.gd, and the
